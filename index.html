The filter

Every product has to make life genuinely better.

Growth can mean money, reach, usage, or speed. Sugrowth uses a stricter definition: growth should be useful, healthy, and sustainable for the person using the product.

01

Give time back

Products should reduce reflex loops and help people make deliberate choices instead of stealing another minute by default.

02

Keep meaning intact

Useful software should preserve intent. Whether it is attention or language, the core job is to help people move without losing what matters.

03

Build for trust

Data, subscriptions, and automation should be obvious to the user. Quiet, understandable systems beat clever systems that feel slippery.

Textezi

Keep the language, change the script. Textezi converts supported native-script text into natural English-letter output without translating the meaning.

  • Designed for Hindi, Punjabi, Urdu, and broader script support.
  • Local-first core conversion with optional AI assist.
  • Manual, share, and Android automation workflows.

How Sugrowth builds

Small products, sharp promises, durable foundations.

As an indie company, Sugrowth wins by choosing specific problems and finishing the details that make a product feel dependable in daily use.

01

Start with a behavior

Each product begins with a daily friction: endless scrolling, awkward script conversion, or another pattern worth fixing.

02

Make the default humane

The best feature is often the constraint that keeps the user in control: local data, explicit automation, clear limits, and no surprise behavior.

03

Ship the system, not just the screen

Brand, privacy, support, docs, public repos, and product websites are part of the product experience, not afterthoughts.
